Whether you're heading to the airport for an early-morning flight, chasing waves on the Pacific coast, or venturing north to the Petén jungle, every shuttle includes a bilingual driver and hotel pickup where available — so you can travel comfortably and confidently from day one.

## What's Included on Every Shuttle

Every Ela Tours Travel shuttle comes with the following as standard:

- **Shared shuttle seat** on a comfortable, modern vehicle
- **Bilingual driver** fluent in English and Spanish
- **Hotel pickup** from your accommodation in Antigua, where available
- **Booking confirmation** sent via WhatsApp and email
