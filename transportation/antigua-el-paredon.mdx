El Paredón is a peaceful Pacific surf village known for its black-sand beaches, sea turtle nesting, and laid-back atmosphere — a welcome contrast to the cobblestone streets of Antigua. The Antigua to El Paredón shuttle departs twice daily and takes approximately 2.5–3 hours, making it an easy and affordable escape to the coast.
