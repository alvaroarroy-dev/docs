Guatemala's rich culture is best experienced hands-on. Ela Tours Travel connects you with local artisans, farmers, and chefs for workshops that go beyond sightseeing — you'll leave with a new skill and a deeper connection to Guatemalan life. From the coffee farms outside Antigua to the weaving traditions of Lake Atitlán, these experiences are designed to be as memorable as any landmark you'll visit.

## Workshop Experiences

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card
    title="Coffee Tour & Workshop"
    icon="mug-hot"
  >
    Visit a working finca (coffee farm) near Antigua to learn how coffee is grown, harvested, processed, and roasted. Includes a guided tasting session so you can appreciate Guatemala's world-renowned coffee from seed to cup.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Traditional Weaving"
    icon="shirt"
  >
    Learn the ancient Maya backstrap loom technique from local weavers in the Lake Atitlán region. You'll create your own small textile to take home — a one-of-a-kind souvenir made by your own hands.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Chocolate Making"
    icon="candy-bar"
  >
    Discover cacao's deep roots in Mesoamerican history and make your own Guatemalan chocolate from bean to bar. You'll grind, mix, and mold chocolate using traditional methods passed down through generations.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Cooking Class"
    icon="utensils"
  >
    Cook traditional Guatemalan dishes — pepián, tamales, and fresh tortillas — guided by a local chef in an Antigua kitchen. Learn the flavors and techniques behind Guatemala's most beloved recipes and enjoy the meal you've made.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Workshop Details

All of Ela Tours Travel's workshops are designed to be immersive, personal, and accessible to everyone — no prior experience required:

- **All workshops include bilingual instruction** (English/Spanish), hands-on participation, and take-home products or recipes
- **Group sizes are kept small** to ensure a personal, unhurried experience
- **Duration varies by workshop** — most run between 2 and 4 hours
- **Available for individuals, couples, families, and private groups** — we tailor the experience to your party
