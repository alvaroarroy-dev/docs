## What Every Tour Includes

Every guided experience with Ela Tours Travel is built around quality, safety, and genuine local connection. All tours come with:

- **Expert bilingual guide** (English/Spanish)
- **Small-group or private options** so you get personal attention
- **Safe and vetted transportation** to and from your starting point
- **Booking confirmation via WhatsApp and email** so you're never left guessing

## How to Book a Tour

Booking a tour is quick and straightforward. Visit our [Booking Guide](/booking-guide) for a full walkthrough of the reservation process. You can also reach us directly on **WhatsApp at +502 3065 4580** or by email at **reservations@elatourstravel.com** — our bilingual team is available to answer questions, check availability, and confirm your reservation, typically within a few hours of your request.
