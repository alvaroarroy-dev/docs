This guide covers practical tips to ensure your shuttle or tour booking goes smoothly — from gathering the right information before you submit your request, to knowing exactly what to do when your travel day arrives. A little preparation upfront means faster confirmation and a stress-free departure.

## What You'll Need to Book

Have the following details ready before you fill out the reservation form. Providing complete information upfront speeds up the availability check and gets your confirmation to you faster.

- **Full name** — as you'd like it to appear on the driver's manifest
- **Travel date** — the specific day you plan to depart
- **Departure time preference** — your ideal departure window (check available times for your route)
- **Number of passengers** — including any children traveling with you
- **Pickup address or hotel name in Antigua** — used to coordinate your pickup point
- **WhatsApp number or email address** — so the team can send your payment link and confirmation

## Pickup Coordination

Ela Tours Travel coordinates the closest available pickup point to your location, and door-to-door hotel pickup is available on most routes across Antigua. If your exact address isn't serviceable, the team will assign you the nearest designated pickup point and communicate it clearly in your confirmation.

<Tip>
  Share your hotel name and room number when booking so we can coordinate the closest pickup point for you.
</Tip>

## Shared Shuttle vs. Private Transfer

Not sure which service is right for your trip? Here's a quick comparison.

<Tabs>

</Tabs>

## On Travel Day

Follow these steps on the day of your departure to ensure everything goes smoothly.

1. **Be ready 10 minutes before your scheduled departure time.** Shared shuttles operate on fixed schedules and cannot wait for late arrivals.
2. **Wait at your confirmed pickup point.** Your confirmation message will specify the exact location — stay visible and accessible.
3. **Have your booking confirmation handy.** Keep your WhatsApp or email confirmation accessible on your phone in case the driver needs to verify your booking.
4. **The driver will have your name on the manifest.** You don't need to print anything — your name is registered with the operator before departure.
