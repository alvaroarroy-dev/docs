Ela Tours Travel organizes guided volcano hikes with experienced, safety-certified bilingual guides — whether you want to see glowing lava at Pacaya or spend a night above the clouds on Acatenango. Every hike departs from Antigua with vetted transportation and a guide who knows the terrain inside and out.

The sunrise from the summit, breaking above a sea of clouds with Fuego rumbling below, is a sight you won't soon forget.

## Preparation Tips

<Warning>
  Acatenango is a strenuous hike with significant elevation gain. Be in good physical condition and consult your doctor if you have heart or respiratory conditions.
</Warning>

Come prepared to make the most of your hike:

- **Wear layers** — temperatures near the summit can drop below freezing at night, especially on Acatenango
- **Bring sunscreen and sunglasses** — UV exposure is intense at high elevations
- **Hiking boots are strongly recommended** — the terrain is rocky and uneven on both volcanoes
- **A minimum fitness level is required for Acatenango** — regular walking or light cardio training beforehand is advised
