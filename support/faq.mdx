Here are answers to the most common questions travelers have about booking and traveling with Ela Tours Travel. If you don't find what you're looking for, contact us via WhatsApp (+502 3065 4580) or email (reservations@elatourstravel.com) and we'll be happy to help.

## Booking & Reservations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I make a reservation?">
    Fill out the reservation form at [elatourstravel.com/book](https://elatourstravel.com/book), or contact us directly via WhatsApp (+502 3065 4580) or email (reservations@elatourstravel.com). Once availability is confirmed, you'll receive a payment link to complete your booking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How quickly will I receive a confirmation?">
    Most reservations are confirmed within a few hours of submitting your request. Once payment is processed through recurrente.com, you'll receive your booking confirmation via WhatsApp and email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I book for a group?">
    Yes — shuttles accommodate groups of any size. Contact us via WhatsApp or email for group inquiries and pricing, and we'll be happy to coordinate your trip.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to book in advance?">
    We recommend booking at least 36 hours in advance to secure your preferred departure time. Last-minute bookings (under 36 hours before departure) are possible but subject to availability — we'll confirm your spot before requesting payment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payment

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I pay?">
    Payment is processed securely through [recurrente.com](https://www.recurrente.com). After availability is confirmed, you'll receive a payment link via WhatsApp or email. Follow the link to complete your payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What currencies are accepted?">
    Prices are listed in USD and Guatemalan Quetzales (Q). Check with recurrente.com at checkout for the accepted payment methods and currency options available to you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is payment required upfront?">
    For bookings made 36+ hours in advance, full payment is required to confirm your reservation. For bookings made under 36 hours before departure, we verify availability first and then send you the payment link — so you only pay once your seat is confirmed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Pickup & Travel Day

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where will I be picked up?">
    We coordinate pickup from your hotel or accommodation in Antigua. If door-to-door pickup isn't available at your specific address, we'll assign the nearest available pickup point and communicate the details when your booking is confirmed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What time should I be ready?">
    Be at your pickup point at least 10 minutes before your scheduled departure time. Shuttles depart on a fixed schedule and cannot wait for late passengers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I bring?">
    Bring your booking confirmation, passport or ID, and any luggage for the journey. For volcano hikes and outdoor tours, we recommend layered clothing, sturdy closed-toe shoes, sunscreen, and plenty of water.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Cancellations & Changes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the cancellation policy?">
    - **Cancellations 36+ hours before departure** receive a full or partial refund, minus any non-refundable processing fees.
    - **Cancellations under 36 hours** before departure are non-refundable unless approved through a special review.
    - **Force majeure cancellations** (natural disasters, government restrictions, illness, etc.) may qualify for a full or partial refund depending on transaction status.

    For full details, see the [Cancellation Policy](/policies/cancellations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel or change my booking?">
    Contact us as soon as possible via WhatsApp (+502 3065 4580) or email (reservations@elatourstravel.com). Include your booking confirmation number and travel date so the team can process your request quickly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my departure time?">
    Changes to your departure time are subject to availability. Contact us as early as possible to request a schedule change, and the team will do their best to accommodate you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Services

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are all drivers bilingual?">
    Yes — all Ela Tours Travel drivers and guides are bilingual in English and Spanish, so you can communicate comfortably throughout your journey.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is luggage included?">
    Standard luggage is accommodated on all shuttles at no additional charge. If you're traveling with oversized or excessive luggage, please contact us in advance so we can make the necessary arrangements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer private transfers?">
    Yes — private transfers are available on request. Contact us via WhatsApp or email for pricing and availability, and we'll put together an option that fits your schedule and group size.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
