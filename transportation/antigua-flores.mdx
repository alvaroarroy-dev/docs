Flores is the charming island town in the Petén region and the primary gateway to the Tikal National Park jungle ruins. The overnight shuttle from Antigua departs each evening at 18:30, arriving in Flores after an 8–10 hour journey — so you wake up in the jungle, ready for your morning Tikal adventure without wasting a day on travel.
