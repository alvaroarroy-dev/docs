By making a reservation through Ela Tours Travel, you agree to the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before completing your booking.

## Booking Agent Disclaimer

<Note>
  Ela Tours Travel Guatemala acts solely as a booking agent for third-party transportation and tour operators. The services themselves are provided by licensed third-party operators.
</Note>

This means Ela Tours Travel facilitates your booking, handles payment, and provides customer support — but the actual transportation or tour is operated by a vetted third-party provider. Ela Tours Travel takes care in selecting reliable partners to ensure you receive a safe and professional experience.

## Pricing & Payments

- **Prices are per person** unless otherwise stated on the destination or tour page.
- Payment is processed securely through **recurrente.com** — an encrypted third-party payment processor.
- **For reservations made 36+ hours in advance:** full payment is required via recurrente.com to confirm your booking.
- **For reservations made under 36 hours before departure:** Ela Tours Travel first confirms availability, then sends you a payment link to complete the booking.

## Reservation & Confirmation

- A reservation is **not confirmed** until payment is received and a confirmation is sent to you via WhatsApp and email.
- Ela Tours Travel reserves the right to refuse or cancel a booking if a departure is fully booked or the service is otherwise unavailable. In such cases, any payment received will be refunded in full.

## Pickup & Schedule

- Departure times are fixed. You must be at your pickup point before the scheduled departure time.
- If you miss your shuttle, no refund or rescheduling is guaranteed.
- Pickup locations are assigned based on availability. Door-to-door pickup is offered where possible; otherwise, the nearest available pickup point applies. The team will coordinate your exact pickup location when your booking is confirmed.
