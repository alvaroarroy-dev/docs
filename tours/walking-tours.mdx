Antigua Guatemala is one of the best-preserved colonial cities in the Americas, and a guided walking tour is the best way to uncover its history, architecture, and local life. Ela Tours Travel's walking tours are led by bilingual local guides who bring the city's 500-year story to life — from its founding as the colonial capital of Central America to the earthquakes that shaped its iconic ruined skyline.

## What You'll See

Antigua rewards curious walkers at every corner. On your tour, you'll explore:

- **Central Park (Parque Central)** and the Palace of the Captains General
- **La Merced Church** and its famous baroque facade
- **Ruins of the Cathedral of Santiago**, one of the most striking colonial ruins in the region
- **Local mercado (market)** and artisan workshops where craftspeople work in traditional techniques
- **Secret courtyards and panoramic viewpoints** above the city with views of the surrounding volcanoes
