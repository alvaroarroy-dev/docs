## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your route or tour">
    Browse the [Transportation](/transportation/overview) or [Tours](/tours/overview) sections to find your destination and check departure schedules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a reservation request">
    Fill out the reservation form on [elatourstravel.com/book](https://elatourstravel.com/book) or contact us directly via [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/50230654580) or email at reservations@elatourstravel.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm availability and pay">
    We verify availability for your date and send a secure payment link via [recurrente.com](https://recurrente.com). No upfront commitment — you pay only after confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your confirmation">
    Get your final booking confirmation via WhatsApp and email, including your pickup time and meeting point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Booking 36+ hours in advance? Pay upfront for a faster confirmation. Need a last-minute shuttle? Contact us first — we'll check availability before you pay.
</Tip>
