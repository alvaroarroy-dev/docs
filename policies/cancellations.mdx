Ela Tours Travel understands that travel plans can change. The cancellation and refund policy is designed to be fair and transparent, with clear guidelines based on how far in advance you cancel.

## Cancellation Windows

| Cancellation Timing | Refund Outcome |
|---|---|
| Force majeure (natural disaster, illness, etc.) | Full or partial refund depending on transaction status |
| 36+ hours before departure | Full or partial refund, minus any non-refundable processing fees |
| Under 36 hours before departure | No refund (unless approved through special review) |

<Warning>
  Cancellations made less than 36 hours before departure are non-refundable. If you have extenuating circumstances, contact us immediately to request a special review.
</Warning>

    The team will confirm receipt of your cancellation and process your refund request according to the policy above.

  <Step title="Receive your refund">
    Refunds are processed back through recurrente.com once your cancellation request has been reviewed and approved in line with the policy above.
  </Step>

## Force Majeure

Events beyond either party's control — including natural disasters, government restrictions, severe weather, and sudden illness — may qualify for a full or partial refund regardless of when the cancellation occurs. If you need to cancel due to a force majeure event, contact the Ela Tours Travel team immediately and include any supporting documentation (such as medical certificates, official travel advisories, or news reports). Each case is reviewed individually, and the outcome depends on the transaction status at the time of the event.

## Non-Refundable Fees

<Info>
  Some payment processing fees charged by recurrente.com may be non-refundable regardless of cancellation timing. These will be communicated to you when your refund is processed.
</Info>
