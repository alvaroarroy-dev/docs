## Key Features

- **Bilingual drivers and guides** — All staff communicate fluently in English and Spanish, so you're never left guessing.
- **Hotel and door-to-door pickup** — Ela Tours coordinates the closest available pickup point to your location, including direct hotel pickups on most routes.
- **Secure payments via recurrente.com** — Every transaction is processed through an encrypted, trusted payment platform. You pay only after availability is confirmed.
- **WhatsApp and email support** — Reach the team at any time through WhatsApp (+502 3065 4580) or at reservations@elatourstravel.com.
- **Quick reservation confirmation** — Most bookings are confirmed within hours of submitting your request.
- **Flexible cancellation policy** — Cancel 36 or more hours before departure for a full or partial refund, minus any non-refundable fees.

## Service Area

Ela Tours Travel operates primarily out of Antigua, Guatemala, connecting you to the country's most visited destinations and beyond. Core domestic routes include Guatemala City's La Aurora International Airport, the lakeside towns of Panajachel and San Pedro La Laguna on Lake Atitlán, the Pacific surf village of El Paredón, and Flores in the Petén region — the gateway to the ancient ruins of Tikal. For travelers crossing borders, Ela Tours also runs an international shuttle to San Salvador, El Salvador, with border crossing assistance included.
