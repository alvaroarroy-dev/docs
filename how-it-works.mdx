Booking a shuttle or tour with Ela Tours Travel is a straightforward five-step process built around one important principle: availability is always verified before you pay. This means you never commit funds to a service that isn't confirmed, and the team handles all the logistics between your request and your final confirmation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill Out the Form">
    Visit [elatourstravel.com/book](https://elatourstravel.com/book) and complete the reservation request form with your travel details — including your route, preferred departure date and time, and the number of passengers traveling with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Request">
    Once you submit, your reservation request is automatically sent to the Ela Tours team via WhatsApp and email for review. No payment is required at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Availability Check">
    The Ela Tours team verifies availability for your selected date, time, and route with the relevant licensed transportation operator. You'll hear back promptly if anything needs to be adjusted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure Payment">
    Once availability is confirmed, you'll receive a secure payment link powered by recurrente.com. Complete your payment through the encrypted checkout — your spot is held as soon as payment is received.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Confirmation">
    Your final booking confirmation is sent to you via WhatsApp and email. It includes your pickup time, meeting point details, and everything else you need for travel day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Booking Timelines

How quickly the process moves depends on how far in advance you're booking.

<Info>
  **Booking 36+ hours in advance:** Complete your payment after confirmation is issued. The full five-step process applies, and you can expect confirmation within a few hours of submitting your request.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Booking under 36 hours before departure:** Availability is checked first before a payment link is issued. Last-minute requests are accommodated when possible, but routes may have limited or no availability. Contact the team directly via WhatsApp for the fastest response.
</Warning>

## Payment & Security

All payments are processed securely through [recurrente.com](https://recurrente.com), a trusted and encrypted payment platform used widely across Central America. There is no upfront commitment — you only receive a payment link after the team has confirmed that your selected route and date are available. This means you never pay for a service that can't be delivered.
